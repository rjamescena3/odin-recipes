Beef Shepherd’s Pie

Description

A savory, comforting dish of seasoned minced beef and vegetables topped with golden baked mashed potatoes.

Follow the steps below to prepare the ingredients and bake this dish to perfection.

Ingredients

Filling

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion(chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • salt
  • pepper

Potato Topping

  • 4 large potatoes (peeled and cubed)
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ¼ cup milk
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional)
  • salt
  • pepper

Now Steps to make dish of Beef shepherd's Pie

Steps To Follow

  1. Make mashed Potatoes: Boil potatoes in salted water until tender (about 15 minutes).Drain and mash with butter,milk,salt and pepper until smooth
  2. Cook Beef Filling:In a skillet over medium heat,brown ground beef with onion and garlic. Drain excess fat. Stir in tomato paste and flour, then pour in beef broth and frozen peas/carrot. Simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.
  3. Assemble & Bake: Preheat oven to 2000C (4000F).Spread the beef mixture evenly into a baking dish. Top with mashed potatoes,smoothing with a spatula (and sprinkling with cheddar cheese if using ).Bake for 20 minutes until the top is golden.
